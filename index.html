Openclaw

A personal, single-user automation tool.

Openclaw is a private assistant running on its owner's own hardware. This site exists to satisfy Google's OAuth publishing requirements for an application used by exactly one person: its owner.

The application signs in to the owner's own Google account to read email, calendar, and drive files on the owner's behalf, so the owner can search and act on their own data from their own machine. There are no other users, no accounts to create, and no service offered to the public.
